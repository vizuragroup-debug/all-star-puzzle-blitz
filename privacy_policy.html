Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 22/6/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we ("we," "us," or "our") collect, use, and disclose information about you when you play All-Star Puzzle Blitz (the "Game").

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account with a real name, email address, or password to play the Game. However, to provide features like cloud saves, leaderboards, and analytics, we collect the following:

  • Anonymous Identifiers: When you launch the Game, we generate a random, anonymous Unique Device Identifier (UUID).
  • Game Data: We store your chosen in-game username, avatar, completed levels, stars earned, coin balance, and friends list securely on our servers.
  • Usage Data: We track anonymous gameplay events (e.g., levels started, levels failed, store visits) to help us balance the game difficulty and improve crashes.
  • Purchase Data: If you buy in-game items, payments are securely processed by your platform (e.g., Apple App Store or Google Play Store) and our receipt validation partners. We do not collect or store your credit card information. We only log anonymous receipt validation data to award your items.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Game uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, primarily to serve advertisements, manage cloud data, or provide analytics. These services may include:

  • Advertising Providers: We use third-party ad networks to serve advertisements. These providers may collect and use device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), IP addresses, and generalized location data to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • Cloud Services: We use third-party database providers for cloud saves and global leaderboards.
  • Analytics Providers: We use third-party tools for anonymous product analytics and crash reporting.
  • Payment Processors: We use third-party services to validate in-app purchases and manage subscriptions.

3. Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy > Tracking and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads and select "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Delete advertising ID".

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

5. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the in-game Profile screen by tapping "Delete Account." This will permanently remove your account, game progress, coins, boosters, and leaderboard entries from our servers. This action cannot be undone. If you are playing as a guest, simply uninstalling the app will remove all local data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
